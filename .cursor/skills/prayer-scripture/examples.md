# Copy examples

## Notes

Bad: If you still have sin in your heart, God will not hear this prayer.

Good: God invites us to confess sin and to forgive others, then to ask.

Bad: Believe you have already received it and stop asking.

Good: Jesus says to ask, and He also says to keep asking and not give up.

## Strategy seeds

Bad: Use Romans 8:28 as the seed for "the Spirit helps us pray."

Good: Add Romans 8:26–27 to the library, then seed that reference.

Bad: Trigger intercession on the substring `son`, which matches "person."

Good: Match the whole word `son`, and also include `daughter`, `child`, and `children`.

## What not to put on screen

A strategy may be stored as data for choosing verses. Do not show a card titled "Pray from your place in Christ" with a Watchman Nee citation. Show the verse and let the user select it.
